TED / CONTINUUM — PROPOSED DIRECTION

Let people know you first.
Enter your world again.

It is recommended to make a digital portal centered on personal identity: the outer layer is a selected exhibition hall, the middle layer is a searchable archive, and the inner layer is a digital clone with source and migration. Rich content is gradually expanded through layers, and the homepage is kept simple.

This is a design study record dated October 17, 2026. The new version was merged into the official website on October 18; the plans and stages at that time are retained below. The name, positioning sentence, clothing and image are all first version proposals. Existing public information is used for presentation; original financial documents, private databases and daily work records are not packaged.

02/New information structure

The main navigation only keeps works, files, and digital avatars. About me is integrated into the first screen and identity information; contact is placed at the end of the page. The old page retains the accessible address, and the corresponding entrance is provided during migration.

  1. Home: Meet Ted.Name, real person portrait, verifiable direction introduction. Tentative positioning: "Understand how something works, and then turn your ideas into works." It is a brand copywriting proposal, not a new work experience.
  2. Featured Works: Understanding How You Think.The three aspects are real estate modeling, TimeXmart expression, and AP automation. Each case unfolds background, individual responsibilities, methods, outputs, evidence and open issues.
  3. Complete file: Find the specific content.A common index for projects, articles, images, and life supports filtering, searching, lists, and grids. Keep the complete text of the article and avoid displaying only tags and a summary.
  4. Digital clone: interact with the materials left behind.First display image, identity and public material retrieval; when accessing the model in the future, the source, date and uncertainty will still be returned.

The prototype index accesses 6 public projects, 10 long articles, 41 videos and 14 Instagram post covers, a total of 71 items. The quantity describes the scope of this index; it is not a full social profile or private profile backup.

Content object and release path

Each piece of content is saved with a stable ID, type, title, subject, date, abstract, text, associated items, source, evidence status and visibility. A budget project can be associated with quantity articles, model methods, decision records, and processes; the original Excel remains local. The daily record is first private, then becomes a desensitization weekly report or case upon your confirmation, and finally enters the public memory package.

Facts, self-reports, inferences and suggestions should be clearly distinguished. Skill expansion that is not supported by actual materials should be written as method descriptions, not as results that have been done. Unconfirmed project amounts, customer information and model conclusions are not automatically disclosed.

03/Visual and interactive rules

Warm white paper, graphite black, a small amount of amber; large serif titles and clear text form a rhythm. Live portraits build trust, model illustrations express judgment, and MIRA films express creativity. Three works are selected on the homepage, and the complete content is expanded through the index.

  • The desktop adopts wide white space and a left-right relationship; mobile phones read in single column order, and filtering does not rely on hovering.
  • Unify the details panel, support keyboard close, obvious close button and visible focus; stop the previous video when switching details.
  • Video click play, silent automatic play and blocked loading animation are not conditions for entering the website.
  • A clear prompt will be given when the search has no results; an unknown message will be given when no material is found in the archive search.
  • The formal migration stage adds shareable addresses, refresh recovery, and browser back support for details; the merged version has implemented shareable addresses, refresh recovery, and browser back support for file details.
  • Respect the reduction of dynamic effect settings; text can be selected and links can be copied. Before the official launch, the contrast ratio, screen reading and network performance will be measured.

04/What does the digital clone look like?

The current styling for the second version of the Chinese comic: clear line drawing and celluloid coloring, enlarging the proportions of the head, and keeping the glasses and hairstyle with reference to my photo. Temporary name Ted / Continuum: It not only continues who I am, but also allows for future changes. He retains his thin-rimmed glasses, light brown forehead hair and facial recognition. He wears a jet black collarless structured jacket with a warm white inner collar; a very thin amber line serves as a digital identity signature. The transparent outline layer implies the accumulation of memory and avoids covering the person with mechanical armor.

The three forms share the same set of characteristics: real-life photos are used for professional trust; concrete digital portraits are used for covers and interactions; SVG code tags are used for website headers and light loading. Currently concept art and editable 2D SVGs have been generated, no 3D meshes, bones, motion or sounds have been produced.

The protagonist of this set of images is you. MIRA remains a creative role of TimeXmart and does not replace your personal identity. Later, you can verify the avatar, bust, and small markup on the website before deciding whether to invest in real-time 3D.

05 / How to exist on the network in code form

Deployment and storage methods

The public site can still run on GitHub Pages; private materials and diaries remain locally, and public and private connections are made through exported files that are clearly reviewed. In the future, the model key will be placed on the server, not on the web page or warehouse; any model adapter can be replaced, and the identity and memory files will continue to be retained.

The recommended target is three recoverable copies: a working copy on your computer, another device or encrypted mobile hard drive, and an off-site backup. Use open formats, maintain file manifests with SHA-256, test recovery quarterly, and check formats and dependencies annually. This is a save plan, and no off-site storage is currently configured for you.

W3C DIDCan be used as a future identification layer option, but does not preserve personality.IPFS pinning documentIt also illustrates the need to continuously retain a copy of the data, and the content address itself does not guarantee permanent access.Personal website archiving recommendations from the Library of CongressSupport for organizing files, saving metadata, and multi-location copies.

If memorial mode is required in the future, it will be up to you to explicitly specify the authorizer, visible content, and withdrawal and deactivation rules. Just because you haven't logged in for a while doesn't automatically mean you're dead or have a proxy enabled. Answers generated in the future should be marked "based on files as of a certain date" and do not pretend to be speaking in real time.

06 / Phased implementation

  1. This time: Confirm the direction.Interactive prototypes, reference research, image concepts, public identity code packages. Check whether the home page expresses you like you and whether the three selections are accurate.
  2. Formal website reconstruction.Migrate existing pages, supplement stable detail addresses and return logic, retain old links; verify that all content is accessible. The current version rollback point will be retained before official release.
  3. Daily recording system.Record projects, tasks, quantities/units, inputs, judgments, outputs, obstacles, and next steps every day; summarize them into a weekly report that you can review. Completion cannot be inferred from silence.
  4. Sourced dialogue.After reviewing the memory, access the model to verify factual references, timeliness, isolation of unknown issues and private information.
  5. Embodiment and long-term preservation.Make sound and 3D at your option, configure multiple copies, resume testing and licensing arrangements.

Official version acceptance goals: Visitors find a case related to you in a short period of time; full text, finished film and source entrance are available; there is no horizontal overflow on the mobile phone; the keyboard can complete the main operations; the shared link can be refreshed and restored; the public export does not contain the original business data. These are follow-up testing goals, not measured performance or conversion rates.
